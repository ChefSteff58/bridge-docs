# Bridge: Songwriting Notebook — Privacy Policy

*Effective August 2026*

Bridge is built so that your songs are nobody's business but yours.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Bridge has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of
any kind. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any information about you or
your use of the app.

## Your recordings and lyrics

- The microphone is used for exactly one thing: recording your own voice memos, and only
  when you start a recording. (The tuner also listens through the microphone while it is
  open, and processes what it hears entirely on your device — nothing is recorded.)
- The camera is used for exactly one thing: filming your own video ideas, and only when
  you start filming.
- Your recordings, videos, lyrics, ideas, and songs are stored on your device.
- Bridge transcribes your voice memos using Apple's speech recognition, **entirely on
  your device**, for exactly two things: giving a memo a title made of your own words,
  and letting you search your memos by what you said. The audio and the transcript
  never leave your phone, are never sent to Apple or anyone else, and the title stays
  fully editable.
- If you are signed into iCloud, Bridge syncs your data to **your own private iCloud
  database** so it appears on your other devices. This data is tied to your Apple
  Account, hosted by Apple, and technically inaccessible to the developer — we could
  not read your songs even if we wanted to.
- No third-party services receive any of your data.

## Purchases

Bridge Pro is purchased through Apple. Apple processes the payment; Bridge never sees
your payment details, and learns exactly one thing — whether Bridge Pro is unlocked on
this device. Everything you make in Bridge stays readable, playable, and exportable
whether you subscribe, let a subscription lapse, or never pay at all.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a song or recording in the app moves it to Recently Deleted, where it stays
on your device and in your iCloud database for 30 days so you can restore it; after
30 days (or if you delete it from Recently Deleted yourself) it is removed permanently
from both. Deleting the app removes all local data; iCloud data can be removed in
iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Bridge.

## Children

Bridge collects no personal information from anyone — including children under 13.
Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to consent to, review, or delete.
