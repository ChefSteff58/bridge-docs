# Bridge — Support

Something wrong, or something you want? Write to
**[bridgenotebookapp@gmail.com](mailto:bridgenotebookapp@gmail.com)** and you'll get a real
answer from the person who made the app.

When you write, the two things that help most are **what you were doing** and the
version number, which is at the bottom of Settings (it looks like `Bridge 1.0 (3)`).

---

## The answers to most questions

### Where did my recording go?

Everything you capture lands in **Ideas**, on the home screen. Voice memos are
named "Vocal 1", "Vocal 2" and so on until you rename them — hold a row and
choose Rename, or swipe it.

### I deleted something by accident.

Nothing you delete leaves for **30 days**. Settings is not where to look — it's
the **Recently Deleted** row at the bottom of the home screen. Swipe a row there
and choose Restore.

### Do I need an account?

No. Bridge has no accounts and no sign-in. Your work lives on your iPhone and, if
you're signed into iCloud, syncs privately through your own iCloud account.

### Will my songs appear on my other iPhone?

Yes, if both devices are signed into the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive
on. Sync can take a minute, and it needs a network on both ends.

### What happens if I stop subscribing?

Nothing you made locks. Every song, take, video and lyric stays readable,
playable and exportable — subscribed, lapsed, or never subscribed. Bridge Pro
unlocks the craft tools and unlimited songs; it never holds your work hostage.

### How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by Bridge: **Settings ▸ [your name] ▸
Subscriptions** on your iPhone, or Settings ▸ Bridge Pro ▸ Manage Subscription
inside the app.

### I paid but Bridge says I haven't.

Settings ▸ **Restore Purchases**. That asks Apple directly. If it still doesn't
appear, write to me with the Apple ID email you purchased with.

### Does Bridge use AI to write for me?

No — and it can't. Bridge has no connection to any generation model, so it cannot
write a lyric, a melody, or a chord progression. The tools work on what *you*
wrote. Every word in Bridge is yours.

### Can I get my songs out?

Yes. Any song exports as a plain, readable song sheet, and any take shares as an
audio file. This is deliberate: a notebook you can be locked out of isn't a
notebook.

---

## Reporting a bug

Include the version from the bottom of Settings, what you were doing, and what
you expected to happen instead. Screenshots help. If it involves a recording,
please say roughly how long it was and whether you were using headphones.

## Requesting a feature

Write to the same address. Bridge is made by one person and the list is real —
what testers ask for repeatedly is what gets built next.
