## Bridge Pro

Bridge is free to use. **Bridge Pro** unlocks the craft tools and removes the
three-song limit on starting new songs.

- **$4.99 per month** or **$29.99 per year** — auto-renewing subscriptions.
- **$89.99 once** — a one-time purchase that does not renew.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the
end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged on renewal. Manage or
cancel at any time in **Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions** on your iPhone;
cancelling stops the next renewal and does not refund the current period.

Purchases and refunds are handled entirely by Apple. Bridge never sees your
payment details — only whether Pro is unlocked.

## What never locks

**Your first three songs are free and yours forever.** Whether you subscribe,
lapse, or never pay:

- every song, take, video and lyric stays **readable, playable and exportable**;
- nothing you have already made is ever hidden, watermarked, or held back;
- the only things Pro gates are *starting a fourth song* and the craft tools.

The free tier is the first three songs you **ever start** — deleting a song keeps
it restorable for 30 days but does not free up a new song slot.

If a subscription ends, Bridge does not take anything away. It stops adding.

## Backups are your responsibility

Bridge stores your work on your device and syncs it through your own iCloud
account when you're signed in. **iCloud sync is not a backup** — a deletion
propagates to your other devices, exactly as it should. Keep your own backups of
anything you can't lose: export the song sheets, share the takes.

Deleted items are held for 30 days in Recently Deleted before they are removed
permanently.
